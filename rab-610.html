6.10.1Temperature Correction 溫度修正

A.Requirement for Temperature Correction

The calculated minimum safe altitudes/heights must be adjusted when the ambient temperature on the surface is much lower than that predicted by the standard atmosphere. In such conditions, an approximate correction is 4 percent height increase for every 10°C below standard temperature as measured at the altimeter setting source (Specifically, which corresponds approximately to 4 × ΔISA × Height (ft)/1000). This is safe for all altimeter setting source altitudes for temperatures above −15°C.

【溫度修正的要求】當地面實際氣溫遠低於標準大氣的預期值時，計算出的最低安全高度／高必須加以調整。此時可用約略修正：以高度表撥定來源處量測的溫度為準，每低於標準溫度 10°C，高度增加 4%（約等於 4 × ΔISA × 高度(ft) ÷ 1000）。溫度高於 −15°C 時，此方法對所有高度表撥定來源標高皆屬安全。

EXAMPLE｜範例

Assume that a. the reported temperature is −20°C; b. airport elevation is 439 ft; c. altitude at FAF is 3,400 ft. The difference will be 3,400 ft − 439 ft = 2,961 ft. Through the “Altitude Correction for Low Temperature Chart” for a height above airport of 2,961 ft with airport temperature −20°C, the approximate calculation is 420 ft. Furthermore, the FAF after correction is 3,400 ft + 420 ft = 3,820 ft.

假設 a. 報告溫度 −20°C；b. 機場標高 439 ft；c. FAF 高度 3,400 ft。高度差為 3,400 − 439 = 2,961 ft。查低溫修正表，高於機場 2,961 ft、機場溫度 −20°C，約為 420 ft；修正後 FAF 為 3,400 + 420 = 3,820 ft。（簡報箱計算機內插後無條件進位到 10 ft，此例會得到 430 ft，較保守。）

6.10.3Cold Temperature Airports (CTA) in the U.S. 美國低溫機場

FAA has determined that operating in cold temperatures has placed some instrument approach procedures in the U.S. at risk for loss of required obstacle clearance (ROC). CTA are identified by textual Note published on commercial charting publications. EX: PAFA/FAI chart 10-1W.

FAA 已判定，在低溫下運作會使美國部分儀器進場程序有喪失所需超障高度（ROC）的風險。CTA 以文字註記標示於商用航圖上，例：PAFA/FAI 航圖 10-1W。

Although Cold Temperature Altitude Correction may not be required when flying to non-CTA airports in the U.S. or when flying to CTA airports when aerodrome temperature is above specified temperature on the approach charts, it is strongly recommended that flight crewmembers follow the guidelines in 6.10.3.

雖然飛往美國非 CTA 機場，或飛往 CTA 機場但機場溫度高於進場圖指定溫度時，可能不需要低溫高度修正，仍強烈建議飛航組員遵循 6.10.3 的指引。

A.All Segments Method｜全航段法

Pilots may correct all segment altitudes from the Initial approach fix (IAF) altitude to the missed approach (MA) final holding altitude.

駕駛員可修正從起始進場定位點（IAF）高度到重飛（MA）最終等待高度的所有航段高度。

a.

Correct all altitudes from the final approach fix (FAF)/PFAF up to and including the IAF altitude: Calculate correction by taking FAF/PFAF altitude and subtracting the airport elevation. Use this number to enter the “Height Above Elevation of Altimeter Source” column in Table 6.10 until reaching the reported aerodrome temp from the “Airport Temp.” row. Round this number as applicable and then add to all altitudes from the FAF altitude through the IAF altitude.

修正 FAF/PFAF 至 IAF（含）的所有高度：以 FAF/PFAF 高度減去機場標高，用此值查表 6.10「高於高度表撥定來源標高之高度」欄，對應報告的機場溫度列；依需要取整後，加到 FAF 至 IAF 的所有高度。

b.

Correct all altitudes in the final segment: Calculate the correction by taking the MDA or DA for the approach being flown and subtract the airport elevation. Use this number to enter the “Height Above Elevation of Altimeter Source” column in Table 6.10 until reaching the reported aerodrome temp from the “Airport Temp” row. Use this number or round up to next nearest 100. Add this number to MDA or DA, as applicable, and any applicable step-down fixes in the final segment.

修正最後進場段的所有高度：以所飛進場的 MDA 或 DA 減去機場標高，查表 6.10 對應機場溫度；可用此值或進位至最接近的 100 ft，加到 MDA 或 DA，以及最後進場段中適用的梯降定位點。

c.

Correct final holding altitude in the MA Segment: Calculate the correction by taking the final missed approach (MA) holding altitude and subtract the airport elevation. Use this number to enter the “Height Above Elevation of Altimeter Source” column in Table 6.10 until reaching the reported aerodrome temp from the “Airport Temp” row. Round this number as applicable and then add to the final MA altitude only.

修正重飛段的最終等待高度：以重飛最終等待高度減去機場標高，查表 6.10 對應機場溫度；依需要取整後，只加到最終重飛高度。

C.Correctable altitudes (applicable worldwide)｜可修正的高度（全球適用）

ATC does not apply cold temperature correction to Minimum Vectoring Altitudes (MVA) or Minimum IFR Altitude (MIA) charts. Pilots must request approval from ATC to apply a cold temperature correction to any ATC assigned altitude. Pilots must not correct altitudes published on Standard Instrument Departures (SIDs), Obstacle Departure Procedures (ODPs) and Standard Terminal Arrivals (STARs).

ATC 不會對最低引導高度（MVA）或最低 IFR 高度（MIA）圖進行低溫修正。駕駛員要對任何 ATC 指定高度進行低溫修正前，必須先取得 ATC 同意。駕駛員不得修正標準儀器離場（SID）、超障離場程序（ODP）及標準終端到場（STAR）上公布的高度。

D.Communication｜通訊

Pilots must request approval from ATC whenever applying a cold temperature altitude correction. Pilots do not need to inform ATC of the final approach segment correction (i.e., new MDA or DA/DH). This request should be made on initial radio contact with the ATC facility issuing the approach clearance. ATC requires this information in order to ensure appropriate vertical separation between known traffic. Pilots should query ATC when vectored altitudes to a segment are lower than the requested corrected altitude.

進行低溫高度修正時，駕駛員必須向 ATC 請求同意；最後進場段的修正（即新的 MDA 或 DA/DH）不需告知 ATC。此請求應於與發布進場許可的 ATC 單位初次無線電聯絡時提出，ATC 需要此資訊以確保與已知航機間的垂直隔離。若某航段的雷達引導高度低於所請求的修正後高度，駕駛員應向 ATC 詢問。

EXAMPLE: PANC RNAV (GPS) RWY 15 範例

Airport elevation 151 ft. Chart note: cold temperature altitude correction required at or below −21°C. (Approach chart: RAB page 6-10.)

機場標高 151 ft；航圖註記：−21°C 以下須進行低溫高度修正。（進場圖見 RAB 頁 6-10。）

E.All Segments method｜全航段法（假設機場溫度 −25°C）

a.

Determine CTA temperature limit on the approach chart: Cold temperature altitude correction required at or below −21°C.

確認進場圖上的 CTA 溫度限制：−21°C 以下須修正。

b.

The FAF will be used to calculate the correction to be applied to all altitudes from the FAF up to and including the IAF altitude. Taking FAF altitude (KANSY: 1,600 ft) and subtracting the airport elevation (151 ft), which is height above airport (1,449 ft). Use Table 6.10, a HAA of 1,449 ft and −25°C, the visual interpolation is approximately 245 ft. KANSY (FAF): 1,600 + 245 = 1,845 ft. CARDD (IF): 3,000 + 245 = 3,245 ft. IVANN, TAGER, BGQ (IAF): 3,000 + 245 = 3,245 ft.

以 FAF 計算 FAF 至 IAF（含）所有高度的修正量：KANSY 1,600 − 151 = 1,449 ft（高於機場），查表 −25°C 約 245 ft。KANSY（FAF）1,845 ft；CARDD（IF）3,245 ft；IVANN、TAGER、BGQ（IAF）3,245 ft。

c.

The published MDA will be used to calculate the correction to be applied to all altitudes in the final approach segment as applicable. Taking LNAV MDA (600 ft) and subtracting the airport elevation (151 ft), which is height above airport (449 ft). Use Table 6.10, a HAA of 441 ft and −25°C, the visual interpolation is approximately 78 ft. Add correction to LNAV MDA: 600 + 78 = 678 ft.

以公布的 MDA 計算最後進場段修正量：LNAV MDA 600 − 151 = 449 ft，查表 −25°C 約 78 ft（原文寫「HAA of 441 ft」，應為 449 ft）。修正後 LNAV MDA 為 678 ft。

d.

The final missed approach (MA) holding altitude will be used to calculate the correction to be applied to the final MA altitude only. Taking final MA holding altitude (3,000 ft) and subtracting the airport elevation (151 ft), which is height above airport (2,849 ft). Use Table 6.10, a HAA of 2,849 ft and −25°C, the visual interpolation is approximately 462 ft. Add correction to final MA holding altitude: 3,000 + 462 = 3,462 ft.

以重飛最終等待高度計算，只修正最終重飛高度：3,000 − 151 = 2,849 ft，查表 −25°C 約 462 ft，修正後為 3,462 ft。

F.Individual Segments Method｜個別航段法（假設機場溫度 −25°C）

According to the CTA list, the initial segment is the only segment required to be corrected for PANC airport.

依 CTA 清單，PANC 只有起始航段須修正。

a.

Determine CTA temperature limit on the approach chart: Cold temperature altitude correction required at or below −21°C.

確認進場圖上的 CTA 溫度限制：−21°C 以下須修正。

b.

The IF or All Segment Method will be used to calculate the correction to be applied to all altitudes from the IF up to and including the IAF altitude. Taking IF altitude (CARDD: 3,000 ft) and subtracting the airport elevation (151 ft), which is height above airport (2,849 ft). Use Table 6.10, a HAA of 2,849 ft and −25°C, the visual interpolation is approximately 470 ft. CARDD (IF): 3,000 + 470 = 3,470 ft. IVANN, TAGER, BGQ (IAF): 3,000 + 470 = 3,470 ft.

以 IF 或全航段法計算 IF 至 IAF（含）所有高度的修正量：CARDD 3,000 − 151 = 2,849 ft，查表 −25°C 約 470 ft。CARDD（IF）3,470 ft；IVANN、TAGER、BGQ（IAF）3,470 ft。（同一 HAA 在 E-d 寫約 462 ft，原文目視內插略有差異。）
